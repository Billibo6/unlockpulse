BTG unlock schedule: questions

When is the next BTG unlock?

The next BTG unlock in the project's documents is on Dec 02, 2026: about 2.04% of total supply (407.07K BTG), going to Strategy B, Validators Rewards, Strategy A and 1 more.

How much of BTG is unlocked?

As of Nov 28, 2026, 46.8% of BTG total supply (9.37M of 20M BTG) is unlocked under the documented schedule: 8.5% at launch plus every tranche since.

When does BTG vesting end?

The last dated BTG tranche in the documents is on Oct 02, 2055, 360 months after the token launch on Oct 02, 2025. By then 100% of total supply is unlocked.

Who receives BTG unlocks?

By the project's documents, the largest BTG allocations are: Validators Rewards — 50% of total supply; Community & Ecosystem — 15% of total supply; Strategy B — 10% of total supply; Team — 9% of total supply; Strategy A — 5% of total supply. There are 10 allocations in total.

Where does this BTG schedule come from?

From the project's own published material (exchange announcement, project whitepaper), last checked on Oct 28, 2026. We read the allocations and vesting rules there and compute the dates ourselves; it is not copied from an unlock calendar. Where the documents say “linear”, monthly tranche dates on the launch-day anniversary are our convention.
