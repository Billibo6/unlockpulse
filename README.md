# Unlock Pulse — token unlock calendar

**Site:** https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/ · **Русская версия:** https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/ru/

Free calendar of upcoming crypto token unlocks (vesting releases), rated by size against the coins already in circulation, not by dollar value.

- **Rating:** strong ≥ 10.4% of circulating supply, medium ≥ 5%, small below.
- **Track record:** what prices did vs BTC in the week before similar past unlocks ([method](https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/method.html)).
- **On-chain check:** what wallets of teams, investors and treasuries actually do with unlocked tokens, including transfers to exchange deposit addresses.
- **Alerts:** Telegram [@unlockpulse](https://t.me/unlockpulse) (EN) and [@razloki](https://t.me/razloki) (RU), about 24 h before every unlock of 2%+ of supply.
- **Open data:** [data.json](https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/data.json), [onchain.json](https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/onchain.json), [feed.xml](https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/feed.xml), [strong.ics](https://billibo6.github.io/unlockpulse/strong.ics) (calendar of strong unlocks).
- **For AI assistants:** MCP server — [Billibo6/unlockpulse-mcp](https://github.com/Billibo6/unlockpulse-mcp).

Unlock schedules come from CoinMarketCap and DefiLlama; ratings and texts are CC BY 4.0 (credit Unlock Pulse with a link). Updated hourly; this repository is an automatic snapshot of the site, so changes here are overwritten.

Not financial advice.
