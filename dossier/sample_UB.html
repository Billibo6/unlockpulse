Unlock dossier: Unibase (UB), unlock of 12 Nov 2026

Sample dossier by Unlock Pulse. Data as of 10 Nov 2026: on-chain flows 07:18 UTC, derivatives and prices 08:30 UTC. Every line is marked Fact (measured or read from a named source) or Hypothesis (our reading, may be wrong). Data and analysis, not financial advice. At the time of writing we hold no position in UB.

1. Summary

  • Fact 312.5M UB (3.125% of total supply, about $33.8M) is scheduled to unlock on 12 Nov 2026, 00:00 UTC. That is 12.5% of circulating supply, a "strong" unlock by our scale (10.4% or more).
  • Fact It is a monthly (linear) release, not a one-day cliff. The project's own documents give a slightly smaller figure: 303.4M UB (3.03%).
  • Fact About 80% of all UB sits in five Ethereum wallets we attribute to project allocations. None of them has sent tokens anywhere since we started watching on 28 Oct 2026.
  • Fact Exchange flows over the window are tiny (net 88.3k UB into exchanges on Ethereum, net 76.6k UB out of exchanges on BNB Chain). None of it comes from the allocation wallets.
  • Fact On Binance futures, the crowd leans long (56.2% of accounts) while top traders by position size lean short (45.6% long). Futures open interest is small: about $2.2M across Binance and Bybit.
  • Fact In our archive, 78 of 100 strong unlocks fell against Bitcoin in the 7 days before the date (average -7.3%). This one so far has not: since our journal entry on 4 Nov, UB is up 1.2% while BTC is down 7.3%.
  • Hypothesis The first observable sign of supply hitting the market would be one of the five allocation wallets moving tokens to an exchange or to the bridge contract that feeds BNB Chain, where almost all trading happens. Until that happens, the unlock is an accounting event, not a sale.

7. What history says

  • Fact Our archive: 304 unlocks from 2023 to 2026, each coin measured against Bitcoin over the 7 days before the date (method).
    • Strong (10.4% of circulating or more): 78 of 100 fell vs BTC, average -7.3%.
    • Medium (5 to 10.4%): 70 of 125 fell, average -0.4%. No reliable pattern.
    • Small (under 5%): 47 of 79 fell, average +0.1%.
  • Fact Our archive mixes one-day cliffs with monthly releases. UB's is monthly, so the strong-group figure is a rough guide here, not a precise base rate.
  • Fact UB was logged in our public journal on 4 Nov 2026, 18:31 UTC at $0.1072. On 10 Nov at 08:00 UTC: UB $0.1085 (+1.2%), BTC $74,487 (-7.3% from $80,314). UB is about 8.5 points stronger than BTC so far, the opposite of the usual pattern. The final result will be recorded after the date.
  • Fact UB trades about 30% below its 60-day high ($0.154 daily close, Binance futures). Part of any unlock pressure may have been priced in earlier.

8. Risks and caveats

  • Dates and amounts can move. The two sources here already differ by 3% in amount.
  • Monthly releases may never move on chain on the date. Recipients can also sell privately (OTC) or through market makers we cannot see.
  • Blind spot: almost all trading is on BNB Chain, where we have no free holder list and only about 2.5 days of transfer history. Large holders there are not covered in this dossier.
  • Wallet labels are our attribution and may be wrong. Watching started 28 Oct 2026, so "no outflow" covers 13 days only.
  • Futures markets are thin; ratios can swing on a few accounts.
  • 78 of 100 is a pattern across many coins, not a forecast for this one. 22 of 100 strong unlocks did not fall.
