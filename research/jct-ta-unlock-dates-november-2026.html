JCT and TA unlock in November, says the calendar. The project documents give other dates

2026-10-28 · Unlock Pulse

Key takeaways
  • Aggregator calendars list JCT on November 10, 2026: about 1.29 billion tokens, 2.57% of total supply, about $10.1 million and 12.4% of circulating supply. That is a strong unlock on our scale.
  • Janction's own tokenomics say "1-year cliff, then 2 years linear" from the token launch on November 10, 2025. Read the usual way, the first payment after the cliff falls on December 10, not November 10. The wording allows both.
  • On chain, the five Safe wallets that hold JCT's locked allocations have not sent a single token since launch. Their balances match the project's table to the token.
  • Calendars list TA on November 15: about 25 million tokens, 2.5% of supply, about $1.5 million. Trusta.AI's schedule counts from its launch on July 21, 2025, which puts the monthly payments on the 21st.
  • Neither date question can be settled today. The first transfer out of the locked wallets will settle it, and we publish what we see.

Why the same unlock can have two dates

Most unlock calendars, ours included until this month, take dates from aggregators. Aggregators turn a project's vesting table into a calendar, and that step involves reading choices: when the clock starts, whether the first payment comes at the end of the cliff or a month later, which day of the month payments land on. We now read each project's own documents and compare. For most coins the two agree. For JCT and TA in November they do not.

JCT: November 10 or December 10

Janction publishes its tokenomics with exact token amounts. Total supply is 50 billion JCT; about 23% unlocked at launch on November 10, 2025. Investors, advisors and the foundation have a one-year cliff followed by two years of linear vesting; the ecosystem allocation vests over two years after the cliff; the team has an 18-month cliff.

"One-year cliff, two years linear" usually means nothing is paid during the first 12 months and the first monthly payment arrives in month 13: December 10, 2026. The aggregator date of November 10 is month 12, the day the cliff ends. Some projects pay a first tranche on that day. The document does not say which, so we cannot call the aggregator wrong. We can only say the project's own text points more naturally to December.

The sizes are close either way: our reading of the documents gives 1.23 billion JCT (2.46% of supply) on December 10, against 1.29 billion (2.57%) on November 10 in the calendar.

What the blockchain shows for JCT

The locked allocations sit in five Safe multisig wallets on Ethereum. Their balances equal the tokenomics table exactly, and none of them has sent a token since launch:

  • Ecosystem: 13.15 billion JCT, 0xA68f…9B84
  • Team: 10.67 billion JCT, 0x246D…d562
  • Foundation: 9.00 billion JCT, 0x2503…0E2E
  • Investors: 5.00 billion JCT, 0xE906…a050
  • Advisors: 1.83 billion JCT, 0xa139…DF86

So no early payments have been made, and nothing on chain favours either date yet. The first transfer out of the Investors, Foundation or Advisors Safe will show when the vesting really starts. We read these wallets every three hours; the readings are in our open onchain.json.

Two market facts frame the date. JCT rose about 80% in the seven days to October 28, and it trades about $36 million a day, so the calendar's unlock equals roughly a third of a day of volume. For strong unlocks in our archive, 68 of 100 were weaker than Bitcoin in the 7 days before the date, by -5.1% on average. If the real date is December 10, the November window may simply be the wrong one to watch.

TA: the 15th or the 21st

Trusta.AI's documentation gives the allocation and vesting terms: early backers (seed and strategic, 13%) have a 6-month cliff, then 18 months of linear vesting; team and advisors (21%) a 12-month cliff, then 36 months; the foundation treasury (20%) a 6-month cliff, then 48 months; community, marketing and airdrop pools vest from launch. The token launched on July 21, 2025, when it listed for spot and futures trading on MEXC. Counted from that day, monthly payments land on the 21st: November 21, about 24 million TA (2.41% of supply) by our reading.

Calendars use the 15th. We do not know where that day comes from: the project's documents do not mention it, and the token contract on BNB Chain does not reveal past payout days without archive data we do not have. The size matters too: the unlock is 6.1% of circulating supply on CoinMarketCap's figure, a medium unlock, but 13.9% on the project's own lower circulating figure, which would make it strong.

What to watch

  • November 10: any transfer out of the JCT Investors, Foundation or Advisors Safes. If one moves that day, the calendar date was right.
  • November 15 and 21: on-chain transfers from TA's vesting wallets and any announcement from Trusta.AI about the payout day.
  • December 10: the JCT date by the project's documents, if nothing moved in November.
  • We will update this page and our journal with what actually happened.

Method

Calendar dates, amounts, prices and volume: our calendar on October 28, 2026 (unlock schedules compiled from CoinMarketCap). Project schedules: Janction tokenomics and Trusta.AI tokenomics, read October 28; launch dates from exchange listing announcements. Wallet balances: our reads of Ethereum through public RPC nodes on October 28. Monthly payments are our assumption where the documents give only a vesting length. Base rate: our archive of 304 unlocks, 2023–2026.

Not checked: JCT's BNB Chain supply (about 10 billion bridged tokens) and past TA payout days on BNB Chain.
