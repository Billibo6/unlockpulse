Big unlocks usually drift down the week before

We measured each coin against Bitcoin over the 7 days before its unlock, so the overall market mood is taken out. 304 unlocks, 2023–2026.

Strong unlocks (≥ 10.4% of circulating)
78 of 100 fell vs BTC
Average move -7.3% vs BTC in the 7 days before the unlock.
Small unlocks (< 5% of circulating)
47 of 79 fell vs BTC
Average +0.1%. A coin toss: small unlocks are noise.

What this means: about 2 in 3 strong unlocks were weaker than Bitcoin in the week before the date, not after. By unlock day much of the move has often already happened.

What it doesn't mean: 1 in 3 did not fall. This is a pattern across many coins, not a prediction for any single one, and not a trading signal on its own. How we tested this →

How to read this page

  1. Start with Strong unlocks. These are the ones big enough to matter.
  2. Look at new supply, not the dollar value. It shows how big the unlock is next to what's already trading.
  3. Check the type. One-day lands at once; monthly is spread over the month and hits softer.
  4. Note the date. History says moves tend to start before the unlock, not on the day.
  5. Check futures. “—” means only buying or selling the coin itself is possible there.
